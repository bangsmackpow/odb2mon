ROUTINE CONTROL 0x31 (ID 0x0219)

Register New Battery Replacement

Registering a replacement battery informs the BMW Digital Motor Electronics (DME) that a new battery has been installed. The DME resets battery aging statistics, recalibrates the alternator charging map to prevent overcharging new AGM plates, and stores the current odometer reading in the 3-entry history.

✓ Requirement 1: Ignition ON (KL15 terminal), Engine OFF.
✓ Requirement 2: Same battery chemistry and capacity (e.g. 90Ah AGM).
✓ Requirement 3: Battery terminals clean and torqued to 5 Nm.

CONFIRM BMW BATTERY REGISTRATION

This will execute UDS Routine 0x0219 on the BMW DME (0x0012). It will reset internal battery aging models, restore new-battery alternator charging curves, and stamp the current vehicle odometer into DME memory.

Requirement: Verify vehicle ignition is ON (engine NOT running).
